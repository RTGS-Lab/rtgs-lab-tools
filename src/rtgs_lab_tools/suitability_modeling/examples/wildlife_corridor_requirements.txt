Objective:
Identify suitable locations for wildlife corridors connecting existing protected areas in Hennepin County, Minnesota.

Requirements:
I need to find areas that could serve as wildlife corridors between DNR Wildlife Management Areas and other protected lands. The ideal corridors should:

Criteria:
1. Be close to existing protected areas (wildlife_areas dataset)
   - Prioritize areas within 2km of existing wildlife areas
   - Use exponential decay for scoring

2. Have suitable habitat (land_use dataset)
   - Prefer natural land cover types (forest, wetland, grassland)
   - Avoid urban and industrial areas

3. Be near water resources (watersheds dataset)
   - Wildlife needs access to water
   - Proximity to watersheds is important

Weights:
- Protected area proximity: 40% (most important)
- Habitat quality: 35%
- Water access: 25%

Study Area:
Hennepin County, Minnesota

Output Format:
GeoParquet file with suitability scores from 0-100
